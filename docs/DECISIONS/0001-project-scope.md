# ADR 0001: Bound Telosieve to poisoned intent in a finite service

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-07-29

We will study a deterministic replicated key-value service, three authority roles,
and a finite declared fault budget. We will not begin with Kubernetes actuation,
LLM inference, arbitrary services, or claims of discovering human intent.

This scope permits exhaustive or property-based evaluation, meaningful baselines,
and falsification. The cost is limited immediate applicability; expansion depends
on results rather than narrative appeal.
